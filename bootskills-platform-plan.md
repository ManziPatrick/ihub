## What is Bootskills?

**Bootskills is an online learning and business platform.**

Think of it like a big digital village where different people come for different reasons:

- **Students** come to learn new skills and earn certificates
- **Teachers/Trainers** are invited by the admin to create courses, teach, and earn money
- **Mentors** are assigned by the admin to guide students and interns
- **Clients/Businesses** are created by the admin to hire teams for projects
- **Organizations** partner with the platform for training and talent

**Only students can sign up by themselves.** Everyone else (Trainers, Mentors, Clients) must be **created by the Admin**. The Admin controls who gets access to what.

**It's a catalog. A marketplace. A pitch desk. A learning hub. All in one.**

## 4. 🏢 Client — People Who Need Work Done

A Client is someone who wants to **hire the platform's team to build something** — a website, a mobile app, an AI chatbot, a data dashboard, or an IoT system.

### What the Client Sees When They Log In

Their dashboard shows:
- How many proposals they've submitted
- How many were approved
- Legal agreements (NDAs) waiting to be signed
- Active projects currently being worked on
- Upcoming meetings with the team

### What the Client Can Do

| Feature | What It Means in Simple Words |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| **📝 Submit a Proposal** | Describe the project they want built — title, details, and links to documents |
| **📋 See All Proposals** | View every proposal they've submitted and its status |
| **🤝 Sign NDA (Legal Agreement)** | Sign a digital agreement to protect their business ideas |
| **🚀 Track Project Progress** | See where their project is: Draft → NDA Signed → Development → Review → Delivered |
| **🏗️ View Milestones** | See big steps in the project and which ones are done |
| **📋 Task Board** | See a list of small tasks the team is working on |
| **👥 See the Team** | View who's working on their project |
| **📅 Book Meetings** | Schedule calls with the project team |
| **⭐ Give Feedback** | Rate the final work (1-5 stars) and leave a comment |
| **📄 Download Contracts** | Access signed legal documents anytime |

### How a Client's Journey Works

```
1. Admin creates the client account (client cannot sign up themselves)
2. Client submits a proposal → tells us what they want
3. Admin reviews and approves the proposal
4. Client signs a legal agreement (NDA)
5. A team is assigned to the project
6. Client watches the work get done step by step
7. Client reviews and gives feedback
8. Project is delivered ✓
```

## 9. Multi-Vendor Marketplace Explained

**Bootskills is a managed marketplace.** This means the Admin controls who can offer services on the platform.

> Just like **Amazon** has many sellers but they must be approved first, Bootskills has many trainers but they must be **created by the Admin**.

### What This Means in Practice

| What's Possible | Explanation |
|-----------------|-------------|
| **Trainers are selected by Admin** | The Admin decides who is qualified to teach — trainers cannot sign up on their own |
| **Trainers create their own courses** | Once created, each trainer builds their own content and teaching style |
| **Trainers earn money** | When students pay for their courses, trainers get paid (minus a small platform fee) |
| **Clients are created by Admin** | Businesses come to the platform, but the Admin creates their account |
| **Anyone can browse the catalog** | The course catalog is open to the public like a shop window |
| **Students sign up freely** | Anyone can register as a student and start learning immediately |
| **Admins keep quality high** | They review courses, approve content, and manage all users |
| **The Website Owner runs the show** | They set the rules, commission rates, and grow the marketplace |

### How It's Different From Other Platforms

| Like... | But For... | Difference |
|---------|------------|------------|
| **Amazon** | Education, not products | But here, only the Admin approves who can sell (teach) |
| **Udemy** | Courses | But also has internships, real projects, and client work |
| **Upwork/Fiverr** | Hiring teams | But also has structured learning and mentorship

## 11. How Money Works on the Platform

### Ways Money Comes In

| Way | How It Works |
|-----|--------------|
| **📚 Course Fees** | Students pay to enroll in courses (some free, most have a fee) |

| **💻 Client Projects** | Clients pay for websites, apps, and tools built for them |

When a student pays for a trainer's course:
1. Money comes in
2. Platform takes its share (set by the owner)
3. Rest goes to the trainer
4. Payouts happen automatically or manually
