**Bootskills** is an AI-powered all-in-one learning and professional growth platform designed to serve individuals and organizations across Africa. ## 📖 Our Story

Bootskills was founded by **Manzi Alain Patrick Munyeshuri**, a Rwandan technologist driven by a pressing challenge: the gap between education and employability.

He envisioned a platform where learning is directly connected to real projects, mentorship, and career opportunities — accessible to anyone with an internet connection, whether in Kigali or a remote village.

He was later joined by **KALISA Jonathan**, Co-Founder, who brought operational and technical leadership to scale training programs, internships, and software delivery across East Africa.

### Our Mission

To empower individuals and organizations — whether in villages, rural areas, or urban centers — with real skills, real experience, and intelligent digital solutions. We remove the barrier of location so that anyone with internet access can learn, grow, and access career opportunities.

To become Africa's leading practical learning and professional growth platform, starting from Rwanda and expanding globally. A future where every African — regardless of location — has access to world-class education, real projects, and meaningful career pathways.

## 🌱 Social Impact

### Bridging the Urban-Rural Divide
Bootskills is designed specifically to reach learners in remote villages and rural areas where traditional education and career opportunities are limited. All programs are delivered online with:
- Low-bandwidth optimized content
- Mobile-first design
- Flexible scheduling accommodating agricultural and seasonal work patterns

### Cyber Safety for Communities
Every learner receives training in digital safety, protecting individuals and families from online scams, phishing, identity theft, cyberbullying, and data breaches.

### Youth Empowerment
Dedicated kids and junior programs prepare the next generation with early exposure to coding, STEM, safe digital citizenship, and creative technology skills.

### Economic Impact
Learners gain marketable skills leading to employment, entrepreneurship support enables startups, and an internship-to-employment pipeline creates a structured pathway from learning to working.
