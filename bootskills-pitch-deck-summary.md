## The Problem

Across Africa, there is a gap between education and employment. Millions of young people have access to learning but cannot get real-world experience, practical skills, or job opportunities — especially those in rural areas.

## The Solution

**Bootskills is an online learning and business platform** where:

- **Students** sign up for free and learn practical skills through courses, projects, and internships
- **Trainers** (selected by Admin) create courses, teach, and earn money
- **Clients** (created by Admin) hire teams to build websites, apps, and AI tools
- **Admins** manage users, courses, payments, and projects

> Only **Students** can sign up by themselves. The **Admin** controls who becomes a Trainer, Mentor, or Client.
